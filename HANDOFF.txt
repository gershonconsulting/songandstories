1. WHAT THIS REPO IS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Product / site name : songandstories (experimental CLI)
Live URL            : none. NOTE: songandstories.com is served by the
                      HomeVideoCreation repo (Souvenir), not by this one.
What it does        : Rewrites a YouTube song's lyrics around the user's own
                      story: download audio (yt-dlp), separate vocals
                      (demucs), transcribe with timings (whisper), ask story

                      synthesise new vocals (STUB), mix over the instrumental
                      (pydub/ffmpeg).
